This policy explains what personal data the Vacation Equb app and website collect, why, who we share it with and how you can delete it. Vacation Equb (“we”) provides flight and Hajj, Umrah and tour bookings, travel Equb savings groups and community crowdfunding in Ethiopia. Questions: {{ support_email }}.

Data we collect

  • Account: full name, Ethiopian mobile number, email address (optional), password (stored only as a secure hash), preferred language and your referral code.
  • Travel documents and passengers: names, date of birth, gender, nationality, passport number and expiry date, national ID and contact phone you enter for bookings.
  • Bookings and payments: trips you book, Equb places and contributions, crowdfunding gifts, refunds and payout vouchers. Payments are made on telebirr or Chapa – we receive the amount, status and transaction reference, never your PIN, card or bank login.
  • Referrals and giveaways: who joined with your code and the free giveaway entries you earned.
  • Device: a push-notification token so we can send booking, payment and Equb reminders.

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos or files, and the app contains no advertising or advertising trackers.

Why we use it

  • To create and secure your account and let you sign in.
  • To book flights and packages, issue e-tickets and check you in.
  • To run Equb groups: record contributions, run draws, pay out winners and send reminders.
  • To process payments and refunds, prevent fraud and keep the financial records we are required to keep.
  • To answer your questions and send service messages. We do not sell your data.

Who we share it with

  • Airlines, hotels and ground handlers – passenger details needed to issue and operate your trip.
  • Payment providers (telebirr, Chapa) – the amount and reference needed to take your payment.
  • Service providers that host our servers and database and deliver push notifications, only to run the service.
  • Authorities when the law requires it.

Other Equb members see you only by place number, not by name or phone.

How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account is open. When your account is deleted we erase your name, phone number, email, ID and passport details and notifications. Payment, booking and Equb records are kept without your name for as long as financial and tax law requires.

Security

All traffic between the app and our servers is encrypted (HTTPS). Passwords are stored as one-way hashes and sign-in tokens are kept in your phone's secure storage.

Your choices and rights

  • See and correct your details in the app (Profile) or by contacting us.
  • Delete your account in the app (Profile → Delete account) or on this web page. Your account is suspended at once and deleted after our team's review.
  • Turn off notifications at any time in your phone's settings.

Children

Vacation Equb is for people aged 18 and over. Children may travel as passengers on a booking made by an adult.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above and, for important changes, tell you in the app.
